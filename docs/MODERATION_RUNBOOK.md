# Moderation runbook

For whoever checks the admin queue at `/account/reports`. Check it at least once a day, weekends
included.

## The queue

Items marked **Urgent** are at the top with a countdown. Handle them first.

| You see                                                        | Do this                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spam, scams, misleading, harassment, nudity, dangerous driving | **Remove** the content. If the same person keeps doing it (the card shows earlier removals), **Suspend account**.                        |
| A report that's wrong or petty                                 | **Dismiss**.                                                                                                                             |
| A whole account that's the problem                             | **Suspend account**. It's signed out immediately and everything it posted is hidden. You can lift it from the "Suspended accounts" list. |
| A profile reported as a child under 13                         | If it looks right, **Delete account**. That removes the account and its files.                                                           |

## Intimate image shared without consent (48 hours)

Federal law (the TAKE IT DOWN Act) gives 48 hours from when the request arrives.

1. It arrives either as a report with that reason, or as a **Legal request** from the `/takedown` form.
2. Open the content. If it's an intimate image and the request is believable, **Remove** it. Don't
   wait for proof of identity; removing in good faith is protected.
3. Look at the same account's other posts for copies and remove those too.
4. For a legal request, reply to the sender's email with the ticket number saying it's done, then
   **Mark done**.
5. If the person shown looks under 18, treat it as child safety instead (next section).

## Child safety (apparent child sexual abuse material or exploitation)

Do not delete it, do not download it, do not forward it to anyone, and do not screenshot it.

1. Click **Escalate** on the report. This suspends the account and moves everything it uploaded out
   of public view into private storage. Nothing is deleted.
2. File a report with NCMEC's CyberTipline as soon as you can, using Carza's ESP login. Include the
   account's username and ID, the report ID shown in the moderation log, and the time.
3. Keep the suspension in place. The preserved files must be kept for one year. Do not open them.
4. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first.

If the "Escalate" message says some files could not be moved, tell the developer straight away: those
files are still reachable by link.

## Copyright notices

They arrive as a **Legal request** from the `/copyright` form.

1. Check the notice has: the sender's name and contact, the work, where it is on Carza, the
   good-faith statement and a signature. If something's missing, reply asking for it.
2. If it's complete, find the post or reel and **Remove** it (report it first with the "Copyright"
   reason if it isn't already in the queue).
3. Tell the person who posted it that it was removed for a copyright notice and that they can send a
   counter-notice from `/copyright`.
4. A counter-notice: forward it to the original sender. If they don't say they've gone to court
   within 10 to 14 business days, the content can be restored.
5. An account with repeated removals for copyright gets suspended.
6. **Mark done**.

## Privacy and other requests

Answer within 30 days. For "delete my data" from someone with an account, point them to Settings →
Delete account, or delete it for them if they can't sign in and you're sure it's them.

## Law enforcement

Don't hand over anything on a phone call or informal email. Ask for the request in writing, and get
legal advice before responding unless someone's life is in danger.
